About

A research infrastructure for Indian food-safety evidence

The Observatory is being developed in the Food Computing context at Ashoka University to make incident-level news evidence searchable, inspectable and comparable with official baselines.

The interface is a research prototype. It does not provide consumer alerts, legal determinations or independent verification of publisher claims.

Project objective

Connect evidence without collapsing its differences

The project builds a transparent chain from article discovery to classifier scoring, large-model event validation, entity and relationship extraction, ontology mapping and FSSAI comparison.

News evidence

Provides recent, incident-level reports and an auditable record of relevance decisions. News coverage is incomplete and claims require careful interpretation.

FSSAI evidence

Provides official structured and laboratory-tested survey baselines with explicit parameters, sampling designs and scope limitations.

Indian food ontology

Will provide stable concepts and relationships for food products, issues, evidence and regulatory comparison once validated ontology data are supplied.
