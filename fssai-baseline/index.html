FSSAI baseline

Compare incident reports with official survey scope

FSSAI survey reports provide laboratory-tested baselines. The Observatory will compare them with news evidence without treating unlike methods or scopes as directly equivalent.

No numerical FSSAI findings are shown until validated source data are supplied to the project.

Official baseline

FSSAI milk safety survey following Lumpy Skin Disease

An official survey baseline focused on selected safety parameters in milk following Lumpy Skin Disease. It is not a complete inventory of every adulteration issue reported in news coverage.

Tested dimensions

  • Antibiotics and veterinary drug residues
  • Pesticide residues
  • Heavy metals

Scope notes

  • News reports may discuss water dilution, detergent, urea, synthetic milk or caustic soda.
  • Those issues should not be treated as tested by this survey unless the underlying report explicitly includes them.

No numerical findings are loaded.

Official baseline

FSSAI Edible Oil Survey 2020

An official structured and laboratory-tested baseline for edible-oil quality, safety, adulteration indicators and misbranding.

Tested dimensions

  • Aflatoxins
  • Pesticide residues
  • Heavy metals
  • Refractive index
  • Iodine value
  • Fatty-acid profile
  • BR reading
  • Saponification value
  • Quality and adulteration indicators
  • Misbranding

Scope notes

  • Numerical findings are intentionally omitted until validated report data are supplied.
  • Future comparison records will preserve the distinction between tested parameters and issues outside survey scope.

No numerical findings are loaded.

Comparison framework

Three outcomes, kept analytically distinct

Future comparison records will require a documented survey parameter, compatible unit of analysis and validated news extraction.

01

Alignment

A news issue directly corresponds to a parameter explicitly tested in the relevant FSSAI survey.

No comparison records loaded

02

Divergence

News evidence and the structured survey appear to indicate different patterns after scope and time are made comparable.

No comparison records loaded

03

Scope Gap

A news-reported issue was not tested or was not explicitly covered by that survey.
