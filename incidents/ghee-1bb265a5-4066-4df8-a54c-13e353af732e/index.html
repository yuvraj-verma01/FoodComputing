Article text

Cleaned research copy

SLUG: Crackdown On Adulteration; Indore: District administration officials on Wednesday seized a large quantity of adulterated ghee and sealed the manufacturing unit. A joint team, comprising officials from district administration, food and drug administration department, and crime branch conducted a raid on Shri Ram Milk and Food Dairy located in Palda. Food officer Manish Swami said that preliminary testing of the seized ghee samples was quickly conducted at the newly-established food and drug lab in Indore and the screening revealed âserious irregularities' in the quality. During the inspection, officials found the establishment packing ghee under various brand names like Mother's Choice, Milk Cream, and others. Investigators also found a white-coloured fat on the premises. Primary testing at Talawali Chanda lab confirmed that this was not milk fat but suspected vegetable oil/fat. As serious anomalies were found, the factory was immediately closed down until further orders from the district administration. The team seized approximately 3,400 litres of adulterated ghee, estimated to be worth Rs 17 lakh. Ten samples of various packaged and loose ghee, sold under brands like Kaivalya Fresh Super Desi Ghee, Sabho Delicious Desi Ghee, Dairy Power Ghee, SRMI Mother's Choice Cow Ghee, and others, were collected for detailed analysis. These samples are being sent to state food testing laboratory in Bhopal for comprehensive analysis. "Providing pure and safe food material to citizens is the highest priority of administration. Such strict action against adulteration will be continuously pursued in the future," collector Shivam Verma said.
