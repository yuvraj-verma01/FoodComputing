Article text

Cleaned research copy

New Delhi: A spurious desi ghee manufacturing unit operating out of northwest Delhi's Alipur has been busted and 1,500kg fake ghee and 55 litres of artificial essence packed under multiple brand names have been seized. Police arrested operator Gyanendra Singh, who had allegedly been manufacturing and supplying fake ghee across the city and neighbouring states since 1995. The raid was carried out following a tip-off received on Dec 2 about the unit being run from a plot in Lal Dora, Kheda Kalan. Police contacted Food Safety and Standards Authority of India ( FSSAI) and a joint raiding team was formed. "The team found huge quantities of packed tins, cartons and cans stacked for dispatch. The accused was using ghee-flavoured essence to prepare large batches, which were packed to resemble premium brands," DCP (outer north) Hareshwar V Swami said. Police have registered a case under sections 274 (adulteration of food or drink intended for sale) and 275 (sale of noxious food or drink) of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. "Singh, a Burari resident, has been operating from the current premises since 2014," Swami said. FSSAI officials have collected samples for testing. tnn
