Article text

Cleaned research copy

Noida: District food safety teams seized over 340 kg of food items suspected to be unsafe during inspections across Noida, Greater Noida, and Dadri, as part of a crackdown ahead of the Rakshabandhan festival on Aug 9. The action targeted sweet shops, food processors, and traders. Items seized included refined oils and dry fruits, both widely used in the making of sweets. At Sundar Trading in Itehra village in Greater Noida West, officials seized 150 kg of refined rice bran oil suspected to be adulterated. Another team confiscated 15 kg of raisins and 178 kg of refined soybean oil from Muskan Traders, located in Noida's Kulesara village, after no batch numbers or expiry dates were found on the product packaging. Food safety teams also destroyed 35 kg of adulterated khoya (milk solids) at Namaste Sweets in Greater Noida's Surajpur industrial area. Samples of milk and chenna from the same unit were also sent for lab testing. Inspections were also carried out at sweet shops in Noida's sectors 31, 43, 44, and 52, including outlets like Haldiram's, Om Sweets, Agarwal Sweets, and Bikaner Malaiwala. Officials collected samples of sweets, including ghevar and barfi, and used cooking oil for testing. "With the festival approaching, the demand for sweets has shot up. Many of these seized items, like oil and dry fruits, are key ingredients in sweets preparation," said the assistant commissioner (Food) Sarvesh Mishra. "We are intensifying checks to ensure that only safe, standard-compliant food reaches consumers." In total, 14 samples were taken and sent to the lab. Further action will follow based on the test reports. Inspections will continue across the district in the lead-up to the festival.
