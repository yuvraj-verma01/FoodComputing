HISAR: Seven joint teams of police and food drug administration department, under the supervision of Hisar range additional director general of police (ADGP), raided seven units in Hisar and Jind allegedly for supplying spurious ghee and paneer. The raid was still going on at the time of filing this report. ADGP Shrikant Jadhav said raids were conducted in Pillukhera area of Jind and Hisar, Barwala and Hansi. A large number of tins of ghee, wrappers of various well-known companies and empty tins in which ghee was prepared were found during the raids, ADGP said. The officer said that the premises raided on Thursday included Taneja Milk and Food Products and Rajat Enterprises, both on Sirsa road; Shriram Milk Dairy on Daulatpur road in Barwala; Jog Dairy located at Dhani Qutubpur on Kharar road in Hansi; Jaidurga Food Products, Dairy Tech Trading Company (both at Pillukhera) and Tayal Trading Company.
