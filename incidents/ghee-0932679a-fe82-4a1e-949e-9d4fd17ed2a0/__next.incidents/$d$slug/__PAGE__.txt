N Chandrababu Naidu on Tuesday alleged in the Legislative Assembly that an organised conspiracy was behind the adulteration of ghee used in preparing Tirumala laddu prasadam during the previous YSR Congress Party (YSRCP) regime. Naidu described the sacrilege of Lord Venkateswara's prasadam as "Mahapaap" (great sin).
During a short discussion in the assembly on the alleged ghee adulteration case, the ruling NDA coalition mounted a sharp attack on YSRCP over the laddu ghee issue. Naidu said the issue was not limited to "one laddu" and that forensic findings established the scale of the irregularities.
Naidu claimed that 59.71 lakh kg of adulterated ghee was used to prepare 20 crore laddus between 2019 and 2024, costing around Rs 235 crore. He told the house that the adulteration involved the active participation of officials, technical experts, and a syndicate of suppliers. He said the CBI was close to filing a chargesheet on Jan 23, which was submitted to the supreme court a day earlier.
The chief minister stated that attacks on Hindu temples started during the tenure of former chief minister Y S Rajasekhara Reddy. Orders were issued by the then Panchayat Raj secretary to limit the seven hills of Tirumala to just two hills.

Rebuking the allegation levelled against Heritage, a company owned by his family, Naidu said no Heritage products had been supplied to any govt department to date. He claimed that attempts were made to link the ghee issue with Heritage Dairy with malicious intent. He said Heritage stood on ethics and integrity without govt support. Clarifying Indapur Dairy's links with Heritage, Naidu said the company in Pune did co-manufacturing for many reputed companies.
Speaking on the issue, deputy chief minister 
Pawan Kalyan said that the CBI-led investigation confirmed that no milk was used in the production of the ghee supplied for laddu preparation. Kalyan also noted that tender norms were altered to favour preferred companies.

Kalyan said about 110 crore devotees visited Tirumala between 2019 and 2024, and nearly 48 crore laddu units were sold. Of these, around 20 crore laddus were prepared using the adulterated ghee, based on TTD records placed before the assembly. He described the whole episode as an attack on the Hindu faith.
