Jaipur: A team of food safety commissionerate on Thursday seized 450 litre of adulterated ghee during a raid at D-Mart store in Malviya Nagar under the âShuddha Aahar-Adulteration Par Vaar Abhiyanâ. The spurious ghee was sold under popular brands. The action was taken under the supervision of food safety commissioner Iqbal Khan and additional commissioner Pankaj Ojha. âA complaint was received about fake pro vedic ghee being sold at D-Mart store in Malviya Nagar. A team reached D-Mart for investigation. It found that pro vedic ghee was substandard. There were many boxes of Saras Ghee with the same batch number and series. The quality control manager and analyst of Saras Dairy were called and an investigation was launched. In the investigation, the ghee was found to be adulterated,â said Ojha. The ghee supplied to D-Mart by the distributor was mixed with cans of adulterated ghee. âWe have taken action against the distributor Khandelwal & Company near Vishwakarma Industrial Area, Kukar Kheda. Cans of spurious Saras Ghee were recovered from cartons. A representative of Saras Dairy was called on the spot who certified that the ghee was adulterated. The adulterated ghee was seized and samples were taken,â he added. The team seized 450 litre of ghee. âWe have asked the area sales manager of D-Mart to stop supply of pro vedic ghee and Saras Ghee kept in all the stores and warehouses of Jaipur and not to sell it until further order,â the officer added. Further investigations revealed that Khandelwal & Company had been supplying the adulterated ghee to various stores across Jaipur for several months. The company had been sourcing fake ghee from a small-scale manufacturing unit on the outskirts of the city. The unit was raided by the food safety team and the owner was taken into custody. Food safety commissioner Iqbal Khan said, âAdulteration of food products will not be tolerated under any circumstances. Peopleâs health is our top priority.â
