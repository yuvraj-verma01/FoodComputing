2f:T896,NOIDA: Teams of food safety and drug administration collected samples of palm oil, cheese and mayonnaise from a McDonald’s outlet in Sector 18 and pineapple cake from Theobroma in Sector 104 following complaints from customers that they fell ill after having food from these eateries.
Officials said the samples had been sent to Lucknow and the reports would be available only after a month.
If the products are found to be substandard, action would be taken against the companies. 
“A couple of weeks ago, we received two complaints regarding the quality of food on the FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) portal. A person from Noida, who had ordered a burger online from a McDonald’s outlet in Sector 18, alleged that the food was stale. Our team visited the restaurant and collected samples of palm oil, cheese and mayonnaise – all of which are used to make a burger. These were sent for testing to a state govt lab in Lucknow,” said Archana Dheeran, assistant commissioner (food) II in Noida.
The same day, Dheeran said, a customer lodged a complaint about a pineapple cake she had had from a Theobroma outlet in Sector 104. The customer — a woman — lodged a written complaint on the FSSAI portal and dialled the toll-free number too.
“The woman alleged her health condition deteriorated after having the cake she had ordered from the bakery. We visited the outlet and took a sample of pineapple cake for testing. In both cases, the reports will be available in a month,” the senior official added.
Calls and messages to the two outlets did not elicit any response. 
What if the samples are found to be substandard?
“According to procedure, the company that made the product would be sent a notice and asked to explain why their food failed the 
quality test. If the eatery is not happy with the test result, it can appeal for further testing of the sample at a central govt lab. But if the samples are found to be of poor quality again, the company would be slapped with a hefty penalty,” said Nitin Madan, additional district magistrate.
