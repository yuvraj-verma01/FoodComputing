- News - City News - bhopal News - Bhopal second safest when it comes to eating outside Trending This story is from June 10, 2023 The Food Safety Index released by the Food Safety & Standard of India (FSSAI) has ranked Bhopal second after Coimbatore among 260 districts from across the country. Madhya Pradesh also has improved its position from 7th to 4th rank with regards to food safety. Action taken against food adulteration under âMilavat Se Mukti Abhiyanâ, a state-wide campaign since Nov 2020 Now the question that haunts every resident in the state capital is, "Is the food that we get to eat outside our homes in Bhopal as safe and hygienic as it seems from the results of the Food Safety Index? How much effort put in by the food safety department are responsible for inculcating a sense of responsibility among the producers and consumers both towards quality of food and hygienic surroundings in which it is cooked and eaten?" "I don't know how much effort the food safety department is putting to ensure quality of food served to the consumers but there has been a lot more awareness among people on quality and hygiene at the eateries they visit - be it roadside vendors or restaurants and hotels particularly after the Covid pandemic. As consumers are getting more aware, vendors and restaurant owners are also working to keep their place clean, serve food in clean utensils or use-and-throw eco-friendly plates and bowls. The change is perceptible whether you go to a small tea stall or a big restaurant", said Dileep Jain, who loves street food. Dileep's friend Rajeev, however, felt that the quality of oil used by the vendors is still suspect and even if they are using good oil, they use it multiple times, which is injurious to health. Besides, you can see restaurant/hotel owners buying the "cheapest leftover" vegetables from the weekly haat (market) which is cooked adding spices and other things to make it tasty. When they use soiled vegetables, you can imagine what spices they could be using. As far as hygiene is concerned, raids by the food safety department have found unhygienic conditions in the kitchen at some of the best restaurants and hotels in the city", he added. Sparsh Dwivedi, who runs the "I Love Bhopal" campaign, shared a social media post on Friday referring to Bhopal's second position in the Food Safety Index saying that not only natural beauty, modern amenities and Bhopali hospitality, you have the best food also at Bhopal, appealing to people to land at Bhopal to relish all this. Sparsh, when asked whether he is sure that food in Bhopal is so good in terms of quality and hygiene, said "There is of course room for improvement but food in restaurants and hotels is good. Whether you eat at the restaurant or order online, you get fresh food. They are particular about hygiene as well. Packaging is good. Yes, street food vendors need to be properly organised and sensitised. You need to have a place like "Chaupati" for street food lovers and the quality of food sold at roadside eateries also need to be examined by authorities especially - the oil they use, hygiene and such things." People may not know exactly what the food safety department do, but officials say that they work round the year to create awareness both among the producers as well as consumers for food safety and hygiene. "It's during the festival season that raids and seizure of fake mawa or other milk products make headlines, but we keep working round the year to ensure that food safety norms are met," said a food safety official. Senior food safety officer, D K Verma, when asked about activities of his department, said "We work to create awareness among the consumers to see for themselves that they get good quality food, grocery, drugs, food supplements, vegetables, fruits and other such things from the market. "Swastha Thali, Swachh Thali '' or "Eat Right" campaign is part of our endeavour in this regard. We also encourage them to get milk and other things tested for purity in the mobile labs we have, if they have any doubt or lodge a complaint with us. We also create awareness among the vendors and eateries about hygiene and quality in keeping with FSSAI standards. We have imparted training to 100 vendors on how to use gloves or aprons, not to use the same oil more than two to three times. We propose to give training to 800 more vendors in Bhopal division, we enlist the help of NGOs empanelled with FSSAI in this work and then there is regulatory/enforcement aspect of our work. Our drive against adulteration has been continuing and in serious offences we have booked traders and manufacturers under NSA as well", he said. End of Article FOLLOW US ON SOCIAL MEDIA
