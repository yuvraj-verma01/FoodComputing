41:T585,Delhi Police arrests two for selling adulterated desi ghee
The special staff of Delhi Police arrested two culprits for allegedly selling adulterated desi ghee in outer region. A total of 1,259 litres of adulterated desi ghee of different brands have been seized by the cops.
The Delhi Police busted a racket of selling adulterated desi ghee in the outer district region and arrested two accused.
Both the culprits have been arrested by the special staff of Delhi Police for selling impure desi ghee in the name of different brands.
The accused named Anshul Bansal and Arjun Kumar have been nabbed by the police and a total of 1,259 litres of adulterated desi ghee of different brands have been seized.
Delhi Police recovered adulterated 289 Kg of Madhusudhan desi ghee, 689 Kg of Paras desi ghee, 10 Kg of Milk food desi ghee, adulterated 225 Kg of Mother dairy desi ghee, Adulterated 45 Kg of Patanjali desi ghee and one Mahindra Bolero Pick-up Car.
The police made the arrest on the basis of the complaint registered by Jitender Singh, SMC Food Ltd and VRS food Ltd marketing intelligence officer.
