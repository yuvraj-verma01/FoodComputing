Article text

Cleaned research copy

Indore: The food security department has seized 600 kg of suspected substandard food items from a shop during a raid on Tuesday. The operation was carried out under the directives of collector Shivam Verma as part of an ongoing campaign to ensure public health safety. A team raided the shop, Muskan Dairy, Namkeen & Sweets, located in Chhota Bangarda area. During the inspection, officials discovered that food items were prepared in extremely unhygienic and filthy conditions. There were also significant concerns regarding the quality and purity of the ingredients used in the preparation of sweets and dairy products. Acting on suspicion of adulteration, the team seized 150 kg of paneer, 300 kg of cream and 150 kg of ghee. In addition to the seizure, the officials collected samples of paneer, cream and oil for detailed laboratory analysis. The inspection also revealed that the establishment did not comply with the mandatory terms and conditions of its food license. As a result of these findings, the authorities ordered immediate closure of the establishment. It will remain shut until all necessary sanitary improvements are made and the owners demonstrate full compliance with food safety regulations. Verma emphasised that the drive against adulteration will continue across the district to protect consumers from health hazards.
