53:T692,Pithoragarh/Haridwar: The adjudicating officer/ADM court in Pithoragarh imposed a fine of Rs 1 lakh on Patanjali Ayurved Ltd, Ahmadnagar, for allegedly selling substandard cow ghee. Additionally, the court levied fines of Rs 25,000 on distributor Brahma Agencies and Rs 15,000 on retailer Karan General Store.
The case dates back to 20 Oct 2020, when a food security officer collected a ghee sample from the store at Kashani in Pithoragarh. The purchase bill identified Brahma Agencies, Dharchula Road, Pithoragarh, and Patanjali Ayurved Ltd, Ahmadnagar, Maharashtra, as parties involved.
The sample was sent to the state food and drug testing laboratory in Rudrapur and was found to be substandard, according to RK Sharma, assistant commissioner of Food Security and Drug Administration. The department subsequently informed Patanjali in 2021.
On 15 Oct 2021, Patanjali requested testing by a central agency and deposited Rs 5,000 for the procedure. The sample was sent to the central food testing agency in Ghaziabad the next day, and the agency confirmed in Nov 2021 that the cow ghee did not meet quality standards, according to Sharma.
Following a review of the report, the department filed a case in Feb 2022 under the 
Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, issuing a notice to Patanjali. After hearings, the court on Thursday directed fines and cautioned the company to comply strictly with the Act's standards.
An official of Patanjali has rebutted the charges levelled against its product, cow ghee, in the Pithoragarh court order. He said that the order was âdefective and illegal.'
