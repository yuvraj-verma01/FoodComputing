Article text

Cleaned research copy

Nagpur: Following a complaint about fake oil with labels and packaging of branded companies being sold, crime branch sleuths conducted a raid on Friday at a godown on Outer Ring Road in Hudkeshwar. Cops found a factory where local oil was being filled in bottles with labels of branded companies. According to police, Anup Kolap (48), who hails from Mumbai, is currently working as a production inspector for branded oil companies. He received information that fake oil with labels of companies was being sold. So, he informed the crime branch, after which the raid was conducted. Police found 28 cans of oil, thousands of labels of branded companies, drums of oil, and a transport vehicle, all worth Rs7.6 lakh. Police seized all the material and even sent samples of oil for chemical analysis. Cops have booked Nilesh Shahu, a resident of Hudkeshwar. Cops also learnt that he supplied the fake oil to some of the shopkeepers and took Rs1.5 lakh, which was also seized. The accused have been booked under relevant sections of BNS and the Copyright Act. Cops have not arrested the accused as it is a bailable offence but are questioning him.
